Libya's Acting Minister of Foreign Affairs Meets Qatar's Ambassador

Doha, September 13, 2026

HE Acting Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Government of National Unity of the State of Libya, Al Taher Salem Al Baour, met with HE Ambassador of the State of Qatar to Libya, Dr. Khalid Mohammed bin Zaben Al Dosari.

During the meeting, they discussed bilateral cooperation relations between the two countries. 

Our Mission

Adopt well-established policies that achieve the aspirations and interests of the people of Qatar and the strategic interests of the State.
