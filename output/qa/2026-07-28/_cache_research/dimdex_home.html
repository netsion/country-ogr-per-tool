    DIMDEX 2026: “A Global Hub for Defence Innovations – Invest in Possibilities to Shape a Secure Tomorrow”

    Step into DIMDEX, a world-class showcase of technology, maritime, and defence capabilities that brings together high-profile officials, industry leaders, experts, and key decision-makers across the defence and security sectors. Over four days, experience a dynamic platform for cutting-edge technologies, valuable insights, strategic partnerships, and high-impact agreements.

    More than just an exhibition, DIMDEX is a hub of innovation, collaboration, and opportunity. From exclusive networking with leading industry professionals and serving as a catalyst for commercial and investment growth, to the live signing of Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs), it is here that the future of maritime and defence is shaped.

    DISCOVER MORE

    • His Excellency Sheikh Saoud bin Abdulrahman Al Thani

      Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of State for Defence Affairs

      In today’s evolving geopolitical landscape, fostering strong partnerships and close cooperation is more vital than ever to effectively address emerging challenges both regionally and globally.

      The State of Qatar has consistently played a pivotal role in promoting peace and stability at both regional and international levels. Through dedicated efforts, innovative initiatives, and the facilitation of dialogue, Qatar has hosted events that foster mutual understanding and constructive engagement.

      As part of Qatar’s ongoing commitment to supporting the pillars of global security and stability, the country will host the ninth edition of the Doha International Maritime Defence Exhibition and Conference (DIMDEX 2026). This prestigious event has established itself as one of the most highly anticipated gatherings on the global defence calendar, offering a valuable platform for advancing collaboration among stakeholders, forging strategic partnerships and alliances that serve the interests of nations and peoples, and promoting development across economic, security, and social dimensions.

      Building on a legacy of success, DIMDEX continues to grow in stature with each edition. Over the years, the show has evolved into a multifaceted arena that extends beyond maritime defence, serving as a hub for knowledge exchange, experience sharing, and the establishment of partnerships that strengthen the principles of comprehensive and sustainable security. Experience has demonstrated that global security is a collective responsibility and an essential pillar for the development and progress of nations and communities.

      We look forward to welcoming you to DIMDEX 2026 and to leveraging the important opportunities it provides in terms of business development, building partnerships, and contributing to shaping a future where our generations enjoy security and prosperity.

    • Staff Major General (Navy) Abdulbaqi Saleh Al-Ansari

      Chairman of the Organising Committee of the Doha International Maritime Defence Exhibition and Conference (DIMDEX)

      It is my pleasure to welcome you to the ninth edition of the Doha International Maritime Defence Exhibition and Conference (DIMDEX 2026), held under the esteemed patronage of His Highness Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, Amir of the State of Qatar, and organised and hosted by the Qatar Armed Forces from 19 to 22 January 2026.

      Over its previous editions, DIMDEX has affirmed its leading position as the largest exhibition of its kind in maritime defence and security in the region. It has become a primary platform for showcasing complementary defence systems across land, air, and maritime domains to address the global demand of building a resilient defence ecosystem. Additionally, the exhibition highlights the latest innovations in technology systems, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence – fundamental components for the success of modern-day multidimensional defence missions.

      In this edition, DIMDEX continues its expansion to encompass more defence and security sectors, along with essential accompanying events to discuss the latest security and defence challenges and propose suitable solutions. The exhibition duration has been extended to four days, in response to the unrelenting demands of international and national exhibitors, providing ample opportunities to engage with decision-makers and delegations, and facilitating agreements and business deals that benefit all participating entities on both commercial and economic levels.

      DIMDEX will continue to host its key accompanying events, most notably the Middle East Naval Commanders Conference (MENC), which brings together an elite group of military leaders, decision-makers, experts, and academics from around the world to discuss the latest developments in defence and security, share knowledge, and exchange experiences to address emerging trends and provide a range of innovative solutions.

      This edition will also continue the tradition of hosting visiting warships from international navies at Hamad Port, offering exhibitors and visitors the opportunity to explore the latest naval vessels and engage with their specialised crews.

      We look forward to welcoming you to a new and successful edition of DIMDEX 2026.
