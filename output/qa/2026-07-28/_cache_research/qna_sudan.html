Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of State for Defense Affairs Meets Sudan's Defense Minister

Qatar

  • A-
  • A
  • A+
استمع

Doha, September 06 (QNA) - HE Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of State for Defense Affairs Sheikh Saoud bin Abdulrahman bin Hassan Al-Thani met Sunday with HE Minister of Defense of the Republic of the Sudan Hassan Daoud Kibrun, and his accompanying delegation, during his visit to the State of Qatar.

The meeting discussed topics of mutual interest between the two sides, along with ways to enhance and develop them.

A lineup of senior officers and officials from both sides attended the meeting. (QNA)
