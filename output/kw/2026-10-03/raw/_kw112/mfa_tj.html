Meeting at National Council for Culture, Arts and Letters of the State of Kuwait

15.07.2024 11:55

On July 14, 2024 meeting of Ambassador of the Republic of Tajikistan to the State of Kuwait Zubaydzoda Zubaydullo was held with Assistant Secretary General of the National Council for Culture, Arts and Letters of the State of Kuwait Musaed Mohammed Al-Zamil.

The sides discussed bilateral cooperation in the fields of culture and literature, holding joint events and exchanging trips of artistic delegations.
